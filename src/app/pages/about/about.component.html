My work

Designing for People,
Building for Impact

I enjoy turning complexity into experiences that feel simple, intuitive, and effortless — by focusing on what truly matters. Over the past five years, I've led end-to-end product design — from early discovery and UX thinking through to polished UI, design systems, and developer handoff.
I've worked across enterprise systems, internal tools, and customer-facing products, partnering closely with product managers, developers, system analysts, and stakeholders to bring ideas to life.

I care deeply about both the details and the bigger picture — balancing user needs, business goals, and technical constraints to create products that not only look great, but also drive real impact in how people work and interact with systems.

My story

Beyond the
Screen

I've always had a soft spot for making things look and feel right — whether it's a plate of food, a birthday cake, or a product interface. That instinct naturally evolved into a career, and today I'm still finding ways to leave my own fingerprint on everything I touch.

Cooking and baking are my therapy — there's something calming about following a recipe, and something even better about ignoring it. Design feels surprisingly similar: understanding the rules, then finding better ways to solve the problem.

Outside of work, you might find me chasing a good sunset, never saying no to fresh flowers, or planning my next flight around the world — never missing great food. I enjoy finding inspiration in everyday moments, whether it's places, people, or experiences that quietly turn into new ideas.
